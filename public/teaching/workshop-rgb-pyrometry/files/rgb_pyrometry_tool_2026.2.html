RGB pyrometry tool

This tool turns the camera photos into maps of the temperature T(r, z) and the soot volume fraction fs(r, z) of an axisymmetric flame. It calibrates the camera with the blackbody oven, with an integrating sphere or from a file, prepares the flame projections, inverts them with the Abel transform channel by channel and computes T from the ratio of two channels and fs from the emission of one.

Before you start

This tool needs a computer. A phone does not have enough memory for the camera images.

The whole tool runs in this browser. The images are read on your computer, they are not sent to any server, and the page keeps working if the network drops after it opens.

Recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari work on a laptop with about 2 GB of free RAM, with other tabs and programs closed. A phone is not enough. ANNAbel uses the graphics card when the browser allows it.

The six steps follow the order of the measurement chain. Subtract the dark frame or the background with the same exposure time, divide by texp and by the pixel Kj, rotate, centre the axis and convert to millimetres, Abel-invert each channel, compute T from the R/G ratio and compute fs from red and green by least squares of jj = fs Gj(T). Blue in fs is an advanced option of stage 5.
